What you will see

The report on the right: what the audit showed, ordered by how much it would change for a buyer; what it could not settle and why; what it did not examine; the claims that hold; and an appendix of every claim with its verdict. Click any finding to see the lines it cites.

A conversation on the left. Ask why a claim was rated as it was, whether something was looked at, or what the evidence for a finding actually says. The answers come from the engagement's record, not from memory, and the page will tell you when the record does not settle a question.

Three things to know

01
It is slow on purpose.

The model answering you runs on the practice's own hardware, the same way a paid engagement does, so a reply takes a minute or two. Nothing you type is sent to a third-party model service.

02
The target is withheld.

The project's name, its authors and its address are replaced throughout. File paths and quoted code are shown as they are, since that is what a finding rests on.

03
The conversation is yours.

It is private to your browser, kept for a few days so you can return to it, and limited to a couple of dozen questions per visitor.
