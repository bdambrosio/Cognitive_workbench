What you will see

The report on the right: what the review showed, ordered by how much it would change for a buyer; what it could not settle and why; what it did not examine; the claims that hold; and an appendix of every claim with its verdict. Click any finding to see the lines it cites.

A conversation on the left. Ask why a claim was rated as it was, whether something was looked at, or what the evidence for a finding actually says. The answers come from the engagement's record, not from memory, and the page will tell you when the record does not settle a question.

Three things to know

01
It runs on a hosted model.

This demo is the one place the practice uses a cloud inference service, which is why it is limited to a public project. A reply takes a minute or two. Paid engagements run on isolated, single-tenant hardware the practice controls, and nothing of a seller's code goes to any cloud service.

02
The target is withheld.

The project's name, its authors and its address are replaced throughout. File paths and quoted code are shown as they are, since that is what a finding rests on.

03
The conversation is yours.

It is tied to this browser, so no other visitor sees it; kept on our server for a few days so you can return to it, then deleted; and limited to a couple of dozen questions per visitor.
