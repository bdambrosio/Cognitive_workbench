Why not just ask an AI model?

An AI model answers the questions you think to ask it. Tuuyi starts from the seller’s side instead: it identifies every claim the seller’s documents make and settles that list before testing any of them, adds your own questions and a standard list of questions a buyer of a small software company should ask, then tests the material ones against the code. You see every claim and every standard question, not only the ones you thought to ask about, and every finding names the file and lines it rests on.

Who signs, and what

Who it is for

Advisers and buyers of software businesses in the $1M to $5M range, at the point where the seller's description of the product is the main thing on the table. A claims review sits a rung below a full technical due diligence: narrower, quicker, and priced so it can be done before the letter of intent rather than after. The same method serves a developer choosing an open-source dependency on the strength of its README; the free demo is that case.

It is not a penetration test, not a review of code quality or architecture, and not legal advice. It reads the code and does not run it. It reports the state of each claim and what a gap would change for you.

How it works

01
Intake.

A short conversation about what you are evaluating and why: what you already know, what would change your mind, for a deal what would move the price or end it, and the questions you want answered about the software.

02
Review.

The claims are listed and frozen before any evidence is read, together with your questions and the standard questions, each written as a statement to test. Then each one your decision rests on is tested against the code and the documents supplied. The others are listed as not tested, with the reason.

03
Check and rate.

A second, independent pass re-reads the evidence behind every finding. Each gap is rated against your thresholds, twice, and looked at again when the two readings differ. A README that promises no cookies while the admin login sets one is material to a buyer paying for that privacy posture, and not material to one who is not.

04
Report and questions.

You receive the report and a private page where you can ask about any finding in your own time, with the working record behind every answer.

Why it holds up

The reading is done by language models, but not by asking one a question and taking its word. The models work inside a fixed procedure that keeps the list of claims separate from the verdicts, keeps every search and every file opened, and refuses a finding it cannot cite. A person reads the result and stands behind it. The report says what the practice is responsible for and what remains the buyer's decision.
