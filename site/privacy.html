Privacy

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics. The demo sets one. This page says where the demo and a paid review run, and what a client does and does not receive.

The demo

The demo sets a single cookie that identifies your browser to it, so that your conversation is yours and you can return to it. It records when a session was opened, a hash of the address it came from, and how many questions were asked. Your questions and the answers are kept for a few days and then deleted.

The demo runs on a hosted inference service. What you type there is sent to that service to produce the reply. That is one reason the demo is limited to a public project; do not paste confidential material into it.

Engagements

What a client tells us at intake and what a seller supplies are held in the engagement file, for the period set in the engagement letter, and then deleted.

Where a review runs depends on the level of service. A paid engagement is examined on hardware the practice controls, privately owned or rented sole-tenant, in an environment created for the engagement and deleted afterwards; none of the seller's code goes to a cloud model service. A free beta review on a public repository may run on a hosted service. The engagement letter says which applies.

The client does not receive the seller's repository. The report quotes the lines each finding rests on, and the page after delivery answers questions from the engagement's record. The repository itself stays in the engagement's environment and is deleted with it.
