Privacy

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics. The demo sets one.

The demo

The demo sets a single cookie that identifies your browser to it, so that your conversation is yours and you can return to it. It records when a session was opened, a hash of the address it came from, and how many questions were asked. Your questions and the answers are kept on the practice's own hardware for a few days and then deleted. Nothing is sent to a third-party model service.

Engagements

What a client tells us at intake and what a seller supplies are held in the engagement file on hardware the practice controls, for the period set in the engagement letter, and then deleted.
