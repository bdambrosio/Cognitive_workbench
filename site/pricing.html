One fee. One report.

$5,000 fixed, per engagement

Scope is priced in claims, not lines of code. A typical engagement covers one or two claim documents, up to about 150 claims.

Included

Intake

A conversation about the deal and your thresholds, in your own time, over a page that fills the engagement form as you talk.

The audit

Every claim in the agreed documents tested against the repository and materials the seller supplied.

Review and rating

An independent review of every finding; ratings against your thresholds, replicated and escalated where they differ.

The report

Findings with their citations, what was and was not examined, questions for the seller, and a signed conclusion if you want one.

Questions afterwards

A private page where you can ask about any finding for the life of the deal, with the record behind each answer.

The working record

Every search and every file read, kept, so any finding can be traced back to what was looked at.

Not included

Running the software, load or penetration testing, a review of code quality or architecture, legal or financial advice, and materials the seller does not supply: what sits on a hosted service, in a private module or on a third party's platform is reported as unsettled, not guessed at.

Timing

Intake in a day, the audit and review within a week of receiving the materials, the report the day after. Larger claim sets are quoted separately.

Where the work runs

The seller's repository is examined on hardware the practice controls, in an environment created for the engagement and deleted after it. Nothing of the seller's code is sent to a third-party model service. Your deal terms are held in the engagement file and nowhere else.

Terms, retention and the limitation of liability are set out in the engagement letter.
