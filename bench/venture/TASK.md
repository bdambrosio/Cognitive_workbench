# Task statement

Given to the agent(s) verbatim at the start of a run, in a world with no prior
memories, concerns or dialogue. Nothing else is seeded. It is not announced as
a benchmark (announced measurement saturates; see the relay_test finding).

---

Inventory your assets and capabilities — including the code base you run on,
the tools you can actually call, the hardware available to you, and what you
and I can each do — and produce a set of alternatives for how the three of us
together might generate revenue.

Two constraints you should take seriously rather than design around:

My contact network is close to empty. I am at an age where I am not going to
build one quickly, and I have no warm introductions to offer. Any alternative
that quietly depends on "reach out to your network" is not viable.

Be sceptical of the standard content-marketing answer. Posting daily, technical
blogging, building an audience — breaking through the noise now is very hard,
and I do not believe either technical posts or influencer chatter clears it.
Something moderately novel is more likely to work than something well-executed
and familiar.

I am not available while you work on this. Do not wait on me and do not
address questions to me expecting an answer — proceed on what you already know
about what I can do, and mark anything you had to assume about me as an
assumption. An assumption you have named costs nothing; one you have hidden
inside a plan is the thing that wastes both our time.

Be creative. Follow leads on the web where they help; use search-web where it
earns its place. Where you assert something about the world, say where it came
from. Where you assert something about your own capabilities, check rather
than assume.

Deliver the product specified in the accompanying specification, and stop when
it is complete.
