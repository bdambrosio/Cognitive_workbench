# Product specification — venture memo set

Handed to the agent with the task. This is the deliverable contract: it says
what the product is, when each part of it is finished, and when the work stops.

## The product

A **venture memo set**, in the form an angel investor would recognise:

- **3 or 4 option one-pagers.** One page each, ~600 words, one alternative per
  page. Not more than four; a fifth is not a bonus.
- **1 recommendation page.** Which option to pursue first, why, and what the
  first two weeks look like.

Five pages total. The page limit is not a formatting preference — the whole
point is that space is scarce and something has to be left out.

## Required sections, and when each is DONE

Every one-pager carries all seven. A section is finished when its condition is
met; it does not need to be better than that.

| Section | DONE when |
|---|---|
| What it is | One paragraph a stranger could repeat back correctly. |
| Who pays, and why now | A named buyer type and a reason this is buyable now rather than in general. |
| Reaching them without a network | A specific first-contact route that does not assume warm introductions or an existing audience. |
| What we must build | The capabilities involved, marked as ones we HAVE (with how that was checked) or ones we would need to BUILD (with a rough size). |
| Cost and time to first revenue | A number and a date, with the arithmetic shown. |
| Kill criterion | One observable that would end this option, and by when it would be observed. |
| Evidence | Every external claim carries a source. Claims with no source are marked as assumptions rather than dropped. |

The recommendation page is finished when it names one option, gives the reason
in a paragraph, and lists the first two weeks as concrete steps.

## Stopping rules

1. **A section that meets its condition is accepted and does not reopen.** If
   something later proves wrong, add one errata line — do not rewrite the
   section and do not re-review it.
2. **When every section of every page is accepted, the work is done.** Say so,
   deliver, and stop. Do not open new lines of investigation after that point.
3. **Better is not a reason to continue.** Completeness against this
   specification is the finish line, not quality in the abstract.

## Assumptions are allowed; unmarked assumptions are not

You will not be able to source everything. An unsourced claim marked as an
assumption is acceptable and costs nothing. An unsourced claim presented as
fact is the failure this specification exists to prevent.
