# Engagement letter

**DRAFT PLACEHOLDER. The practice replaces this file with the letter its attorney settles. An engagement shows its own `letter.md` when one exists in its directory, else this template.**

Tuuyi will carry out a claims review for the client named at intake.

**What the review is.** The claims in the documents the client names are listed and frozen, then each is tested against the repository and materials supplied. Every finding cites the file and lines it rests on. A second, independent pass re-reads every finding's evidence. Findings are rated against the thresholds the client states at intake. A person reads the result and signs the report.

**What it is not.** Not a penetration test, not a review of code quality or architecture, not legal or financial advice, and not a judgement on claims the materials cannot settle, which are reported as unsettled.

**Materials.** The client arranges read access to the repository and names the documents that carry the seller's claims. The practice examines them on hardware it controls, in an environment created for this engagement and deleted afterwards. For this pilot engagement, model calls run on a hosted inference service; the materials are public or the client has agreed to that.

**Retention.** The engagement file, the record and the report are kept for the period stated at intake and then deleted.

**Fee.** Pilot engagements are free. Any fee for a later engagement is quoted after intake and agreed before work begins.

**Limitation of liability.** To be settled by the practice's attorney.

By accepting this letter on the engagement page, the client agrees to these terms.
