About this demo

What this is. A finished claims review of a real public repository, as a client would receive it except that names identifying the project are replaced, in quoted code too, with a page beside it for asking about any finding.

What is different from paid work. The demo runs on a hosted open-weight model and replies take a minute or two; a paid claims review runs on isolated, single-tenant hardware the practice controls, or on a zero-data-retention cloud service when the client and the seller agree.

What is made up. The buyer and the deal the ratings are read against are illustrative, written by the practice for this demo.

Privacy. Your conversation is tied to this browser, so no other visitor sees it. It is kept on our server for a few days so you can return to it, then deleted, and each question is sent to the hosted inference service to produce its reply. Do not paste confidential material.
