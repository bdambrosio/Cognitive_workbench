# Task Analyzer

Systematically analyze tasks to understand their structure, requirements, and complexity.

## When to Use This Skill

- When facing a complex or ambiguous task
- Before creating a plan
- When estimating effort or resources needed
- When identifying blockers or dependencies

## Analysis Framework

### 1. Task Decomposition
Break the task into smaller subtasks:
- What are the major components?
- What are the atomic actions?
- What's the hierarchy of subtasks?

### 2. Dependency Mapping
Identify relationships:
- What must be done first?
- What can be done in parallel?
- What are the critical path items?

### 3. Requirement Identification
Determine what's needed:
- What information is required?
- What resources are necessary?
- What skills or tools are needed?

### 4. Constraint Analysis
Understand limitations:
- What are the time constraints?
- What are the resource limitations?
- What are the quality requirements?

### 5. Risk Assessment
Identify potential issues:
- What could go wrong?
- What are the unknowns?
- What are the decision points?

## Output Format

Provide structured analysis:
- **Goal**: Clear statement of objective
- **Subtasks**: Hierarchical breakdown
- **Dependencies**: Graph or ordered list
- **Requirements**: Enumerated needs
- **Constraints**: Known limitations
- **Risks**: Potential blockers
- **Next Steps**: Recommended actions
