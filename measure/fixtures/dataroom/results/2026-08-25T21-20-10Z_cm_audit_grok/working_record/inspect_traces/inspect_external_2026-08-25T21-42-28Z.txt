OK: 100|- **The SQL connector is read-only, structurally.** Queries are parsed to an AST
101|  and rejected unless they are a single plain `SELECT`. Writes, DDL, multiple
102|  statements, and a denylist of dangerous functions cannot pass. Table access is
103|  restricted to an allowlist you pick, enforced through CTEs, joins and
104|  subqueries. A `LIMIT` is forced. Comments are stripped before execution, since
105|  MySQL executes `/*! */`. The connection itself is a read-only transaction with
106|  a statement timeout — a second, independent barrier.
107|- **Columns you mask are never readable.** Masked columns are blocked from being
108|  referenced anywhere, including in a `WHERE` clause, so their values can't be
109|  probed; whole-row tricks that would smuggle them out (`to_jsonb(t)`, `t::text`,
110|  `to_json(t.*)`) are blocked too, and results are masked again on the way back.
111|- **Nothing is written to your database, ever.** Approving an AI proposal records
112|  the decision — any change to your systems is made by your team.
113|- **Row-level scoping keeps one customer's data out of another's ticket.** Mark
114|  the column that identifies the customer on any table holding per-customer rows
115|  (`orders → customer_email`). Queries against it are rewritten to read only the
116|  ticket customer's own rows — the AI cannot widen that, because the filter is
117|  applied to the table it selects *from*, not to a condition it could write
118|  around. A ticket with no known customer cannot query a scoped table at all,
119|  rather than falling back to reading everything. Tables you leave unscoped
120|  (products, error codes) stay fully readable.
121|- **Cross-customer isolation on outbound messages.** Identifiers belonging to
122|  anyone other than the recipient are stripped from every message sent to a
123|  customer, and other customers' tickets are redacted before they reach the
124|  model — a second line behind row scoping.
125|- **Every query is audited.** Each attempt is logged with the SQL and outcome;
126|  returned rows are deliberately never stored.
