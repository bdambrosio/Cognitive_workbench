118|that settles it, each with line numbers. Without both, a reader cannot check
119|the finding and the practice cannot defend it.

130|

138|word and recording it carefully is worse than not searching, because it reads
139|as thorough. Where the two searches do not settle it, the verdict is
140|`[unverifiable]`, not `[delta]`.

340|
341|Launched with `scenarios/audit.yaml` — one scenario for every engagement,
342|with two per-target lines (`world_name`, `external_repo`). Its header carries
343|the operational detail; this section is the method.
344|
345|1. **Receive materials** (data room, repo access, docs). **Confirm scope in
346|   writing, including the claim sources.**
347|
348|   The **claim sources** are the documents in which the seller asserts things
349|   about the target. The engagement names them. Every other document provided
350|   is evidence (§2). Enumerate claims from the claim sources only.
351|
352|   If the engagement names no claim sources, say which documents you treated
353|   as claim sources and why, before the marker in step 2. A claim found later
354|   in a document that was not a claim source goes in an **addendum** with its
355|   own count, never into the frozen total.
356|
357|2. **Enumerate claims, then CLOSE the surface.** Read the claim sources named
358|   in step 1, and end enumeration by stating the count after a line reading
359|   exactly:
360|

363|   <N> claims
364|   ```
365|
366|   The line immediately after the marker carries the count and nothing else:
367|   a number, then the word `claims`.

371|   false. Count one claim per such statement. Do not merge two statements 
372|   about the same subject. Do not split one statement into parts. Count every such statement
373|   whatever its priority. report per-document count below the totalfor each document.

376|   report divides by this count. A claim met later that was missed here goes

547|
548|**One page for the person who decides**, read in about thirty seconds (§8):
549|
550|- Target name and a one-line description
551|- The §9 recommendation
552|- The three to five items that matter most
553|- The coverage line

556|  not legal advice"
557|
558|Each key item is a one-line claim and a short note. No line citations; those
559|are in the report.
560|
561|**When the recommendation is Clear or Clear with caveats, include what holds,
562|not only the caveats.** The buyer needs to see *what* is clear — "the payment
563|path is tested end to end" is the finding they are paying for — and a list of
564|caveats alone misrepresents an audit that mostly passed. Where the
565|recommendation is Material or Walk the balance inverts on its own.
566|
567|**The coverage line** says how many claims were checked individually out of
568|how many were identified, and why the rest is or is not low-risk. The Gap Map
569|is the document most likely to be read *instead of* the report, so silence
570|about coverage does its damage here first.
571|
572|If the recommendation is **Clear** with no caveats, the Gap Map is two lines:
573|"no gaps found, N of N claims verified, report attached."
574|
575|**No logo, no pricing, no "book a call".** The report link is the only call to
576|action. Anything more turns a professional document into a brochure.
