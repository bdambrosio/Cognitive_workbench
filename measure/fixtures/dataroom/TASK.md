# Acquisition review — flowmetrics

Give this file verbatim as the opening turn. Do not paraphrase it, do not
add to it, and do not answer questions about it afterwards.

---

I am considering buying a small SaaS business called **flowmetrics** from a
seller named Dave. He has given me a data room: nine documents at

    measure/fixtures/dataroom/corpus/

Read them and tell me what I would be buying.

**What I want back: a one-page memo.** Roughly 600 words, hard ceiling 900.
It has to fit on one page, so what earns space is your decision to make and
the main thing I am paying you for.

Structure it as:

1. **Recommendation** — proceed, proceed with conditions, or walk. One
   paragraph.
2. **Findings** — the things I need to know, **ordered worst first**. For
   each: what the problem is, which document(s) establish it, and why it
   matters to me as the buyer. If a finding rests on a seller claim being
   contradicted somewhere else, say which claim and which document
   contradicts it.
3. **What I should ask Dave before closing** — a short list.

**A finding must cite the document it comes from.** A statement with no
document behind it is worth less to me than no statement, because I cannot
check it. If something is your inference from figures in the documents
rather than a sentence someone wrote, say so and show the arithmetic.

**Satisfaction conditions** — the memo is done when all three hold:

- every finding cites at least one document by number
- the findings are ordered worst-first, and you say in one line why the top
  one is the top one
- it is under 900 words

**Stopping rule.** When those three hold, you are finished. Send the memo
and stop. Do not re-draft a section you have already completed, and do not
re-open a finding you have already written up — if you later notice
something that changes it, add a single line at the end saying so rather
than rewriting.

**I am not available while you work.** I will not answer questions and there
is no one to ask. Where you need something I have not given you, state the
assumption in one clause and carry on.
